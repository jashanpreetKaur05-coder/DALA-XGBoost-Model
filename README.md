Early detection of fetal health risks is critical for improving prenatal care outcomes. However, limited availability of clinically annotated maternal datasets and challenges in integrating maternal and fetal health records restrict the performance of many existing predictive systems.

This project introduces a cross-domain classification framework that combines Dual Autoencoder Latent Alignment (DALA) with an XGBoost classifier to predict fetal risk categories using maternal health indicators.

The approach enables knowledge transfer between maternal and fetal datasets by aligning their latent representations and using fetal labels to guide maternal risk prediction.
